# Evaluation walkthrough

1. Open Radiology Agent. Horos starts in the background and the prior study returns.
2. Open Worklist to show arriving studies from Horos and the clinic API.
3. Select the intended evaluation study. Click its thumbnails: the images stay in Radiology Agent.
4. Use Open in Horos when full native manipulation is needed.
5. Ask Astra to draft using the selected template. The report appears as one editable document.
6. Hover a linked phrase to preview its key image; click to pin it. Cycle Key image 1/2 when a phrase has multiple views.
7. Show template import, history, and draft export. The report remains Draft for evaluation.
8. Open the worklist and confirm **Draft new studies automatically** is enabled. After the baseline scan, receive a disposable research study into Horos. It appears as Receiving images, then queues after the quiet period and matching inventories. Inspect the resulting Draft for evaluation and key-image links. Existing reports are preserved.

Use synthetic or appropriately authorized evaluation data for public demonstrations. Live model outputs and the offline synthetic demo are visibly distinct.
